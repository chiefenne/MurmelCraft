- Slices and iso-surfaces on an overview: the preview grid shows at once, and their exact faces are then cut through the blocks of the file in the background (Cancel, Esc) and take its place. A slice reads only the blocks its planes cross, an iso-surface only those whose ranges can hold its value; selecting one reads nothing again, and a slice moved a little cuts the blocks kept from the last cut. Properties tells the blocks a cut went through (#101).

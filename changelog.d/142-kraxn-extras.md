- **Kraxn: Python, ffmpeg and examples.** FoehnCraft gets its own Python
  venv with Gmsh, NumPy, Matplotlib and h5py (or the distribution's
  packages, or none), with Python and the libraries Gmsh loads installed
  first when missing, and a Gmsh start check at the end. MurmelCraft gets
  ffmpeg for MP4 movies when it is missing. KoflCraft and FoehnCraft get
  their examples from the release's source. Offline, a `wheels` folder
  and the release's "Source code" archive take the internet's place
  ([docs/kraxn.md](docs/kraxn.md)).

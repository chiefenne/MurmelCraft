Regions of interest: a box, sphere, slab, cell zone, cell layers along a
patch, or picked cells with rings of neighbours limit what the viewer
shows and what slices, iso-surfaces and arrows compute. Add them from the
model tree's Regions group, the command palette or Alt + R, or with
`region.set`. The Properties panel shows each region's cells, the halo of
neighbours a mesh read in pieces will need, and the memory both take.
Sessions keep regions (format 1.7).

Undo and redo walk back and forth through changes of the camera and the display. The menu names the step, and **Undo history** lists all of them.

<!-- more -->

## Undo and redo

- **Undo** and **Redo** each walk one step. A mouse drag or a run of wheel steps is one step.
- **Undo history…** lists the steps; click one to go there.
- The selection, settings and files are not undone.
- Up to 200 steps are kept; opening another mesh starts again.

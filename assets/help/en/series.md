Open a time series of results and play it, or step through it. The player bar under the viewport shows the time; the range of a field can cover all steps.

<!-- more -->

## Time series

- **Open time series…** reads a folder of results (or a series file).
- **Play or pause**, **Next** and **Previous time step**, **First** and **Last**.
- The next step is read ahead while one is shown.
- Field range **over all time steps** keeps the colours comparable.

Movies can play a series, with its time label.

A probe shows the field values at a point: the cell value and its gradient. With a time series, the probe history plots them over the run and exports CSV.

<!-- more -->

## Probes

- **Place a probe**: click a point on the mesh.
- The tree lists probes with their values; rename them there.
- **Probe history** plots each probe's value over the steps of a series, and exports it as CSV.

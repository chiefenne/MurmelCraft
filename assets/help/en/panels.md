The model tree on the left lists the mesh by zones, cell types and patches, and everything you add: clip planes, slices, iso-surfaces, arrows, streamlines, regions, measurements, probes and saved views. Properties on the right shows what is selected.

<!-- more -->

## Model tree and Properties

- **Show or hide the model tree** and **Show or hide the properties** toggle the panels.
- **Filter the model tree** finds items by name.
- A click selects an item, a double-click fits it in the view, and the context menu (right-click) offers what fits the item: hide, isolate, colour, rename, remove.
- Properties shows counts, bounds, quality and field values of the selection, and of a picked cell or face.
- The status bar shows the cursor's coordinates, the frame time, the GPU and any message.

**Next region** and **Previous region** move the keyboard focus between the title bar, toolbar, model tree, viewport, Properties and status bar.

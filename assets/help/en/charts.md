Charts of a run's tables (mass flow, cumulative injection, penetration, diameters and histograms) in a panel under the viewport.

<!-- more -->

## Charts

- **Open charts…** reads the tables of a run's output folder.
- **Show or hide** the panel; choose the chart.
- **Save the chart as a picture…** writes the chart shown.

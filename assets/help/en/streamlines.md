Streamlines follow the flow from seeds on a line, in a ball, on a patch or on a plane. Draw them as lines or tubes, with arrowheads, coloured by a field.

<!-- more -->

## Streamlines

- **Show or hide**; a seed line is added if there is none.
- Seeds on a **line**, in a **ball**, on a **patch** or on a **plane**; select the streamlines and drag the handles to move the seeds.
- Trace **downstream**, **upstream** or **both ways**.
- **Shaded tubes or thin lines**, **arrowheads** on or off, **more** or **fewer** seeds.
- Colour **solid** or **by a field**.

On a large mesh opened as an overview, streamlines are traced through the blocks they pass.

The injector of a FoehnCraft spray run: its tip as a shaded surface, a line along each hole and the outline of each injector's cone. It stays at every step of the time series, under the parcels.

<!-- more -->

## Injector devices

- **Open injector devices…** reads `spray/devices.vtu` of a run; a time series whose `series.json` lists it opens it by itself.
- **Show or hide**; the **tip surface**, the **hole axes and cones** on or off; **remove**.

Right-click the devices' row in the tree for their parts and the colour of the tip. A clip plane through the tip shows its holes.

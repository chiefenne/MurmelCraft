Click a face in the viewport to pick it, or a row in the tree to select an item. The toolbar's Select group chooses whether a click picks faces or whole cells.

<!-- more -->

## Selecting and picking

- A click in the viewport picks the face or cell under the cursor; Properties shows its numbers.
- **Click picks faces** and **Click picks cells** choose what a click takes.
- **Clear the selection** empties it.

Resting the pointer on the mesh shows what is under it, and the status bar shows its coordinates.

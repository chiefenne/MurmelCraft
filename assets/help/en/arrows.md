Vector arrows show the direction and size of a vector field, such as velocity, on slices or on the surface. Choose their density and length, and whether they lie in the slice's plane.

<!-- more -->

## Vector arrows

- **Show or hide** the arrows; **on the surface** or **in the plane** of a slice.
- **Denser** and **sparser** change how many arrows go across each slice.
- **Longer** and **shorter** scale them. Length can follow the magnitude, have a floor so slow regions still show, or be equal for all.
- Colour them solid, by the field shown, or by their own magnitude with a colour bar of its own.

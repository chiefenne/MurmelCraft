Measure distances and angles on the mesh. Points snap to nodes, edges, face and cell centres; the results stay in the model tree.

<!-- more -->

## Measuring

- **Measure a distance**: click two points.
- **Measure an angle**: click three points; the angle is at the second.
- The toolbar's **Snap to** menu chooses what points snap to: nodes, edges, edge midpoints, face or cell centres, the surface, or a typed point.
- **Delete the selected measurements and probes**, or all of them.

Measurements are kept in session files. Double-click one in the tree to rename it.

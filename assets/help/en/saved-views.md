Save the camera as a named view and come back to it later. Saved views are listed in the model tree, kept in session files, and can be stops of a movie.

<!-- more -->

## Saved views

- **Save the current view** adds it to the tree's Views.
- **Next saved view** and **Previous saved view** walk through them.
- In the tree, double-click a view to rename it; the context menu removes it.

Slices cut through the mesh and show a field on the cut. Slice along X, Y or Z, along the view, as a single plane or a series of planes, and drag the handle to move it.

<!-- more -->

## Slices

- **Slice normal to X, Y or Z**, or **along the view direction**.
- **Add a slice** adds another.
- **A series of 5 through the mesh** spreads planes across it; **One plane instead of a series** goes back.
- **Show or hide**, **Remove the slice**, **Remove all slices**.

A slice is coloured by the field shown. While you drag it, a preview grid shows the slice at once, and the exact cut follows.

Look along a standard direction: front, back, left, right, top, bottom and the corners between them. The view cube in the viewport does the same with a click on a face, edge or corner.

<!-- more -->

## Standard views and the view cube

- **Home view** returns to the starting view.
- The six faces, and the corners between them, are in View ▸ Standard views and in the palette.
- On the **view cube**, a click on a face, edge or corner turns to that view, and the arrows beside it roll or turn the view by 90°.
- **Orbit** and **Pan** commands step the view with the arrow keys when the viewport has the focus.

The views follow the numeric keypad's layout: 1 is the front, 3 the right and 7 the top, with Alt (Option on a Mac).

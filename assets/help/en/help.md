Tips appear when you rest the pointer on a control: first a short line, then more after a moment, or at once while you hold Shift. **F1** opens the tip's page in the manual, and F1 with no tip opens the manual's contents.

<!-- more -->

## Help

The manual opens in the **Help panel** at the right of the window. It lists the pages by chapter, finds pages by their words (Search the manual), and keeps your way back (Back). A page ends with the commands it explains, with your keys; a click runs one. Links lead to other pages, such as [Tips and their delays](settings).

The Help menu also has:

- **Tool reference** lists every tool that the window, the command line and agents share.
- **Connect an agent** explains `murmel mcp`.
- **Report an issue** opens the issue tracker.
- **About MurmelCraft** shows the version and licences.

Tips also appear for the control the keyboard focus is on. Their delays are in Settings.

**Fit view** frames everything shown; **Fit the selection** frames only what is selected. **Zoom to box** lets you draw a box with the mouse, and the box then fills the view.

<!-- more -->

## Fit and zoom

- **Fit view** keeps the direction you look from and frames the faces shown.
- **Zoom to box**: press it, then drag a box over the part you want. One box per press; Esc cancels. In perspective it zooms to the depth of the mesh inside the box.
- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** step by a fifth. The wheel zooms about the point under the cursor.

The mouse orbits about the point under the cursor, so you can turn about the part you look at.

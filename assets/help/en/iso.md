Iso-surfaces show where a field has one value: a vortex as an iso-surface of vorticity or Q, a flame front, a shock. Colour them solid or by another field.

<!-- more -->

## Iso-surfaces

- **Add an iso-surface** of the field shown, at a value you set in the tree.
- **Iso-surface: solid colour** or **coloured by a field**.
- **Show or hide**, **Remove**, **Remove all**.

Iso-surfaces are smooth: node values come from least-squares cell gradients.

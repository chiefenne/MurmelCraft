Make the settings of the selected item the default for new ones: a slice's colouring, the arrows' density and length, a streamline's seeds. Right-click a value to save it as the default.

<!-- more -->

## Your defaults

- **Save the selected item's settings as defaults**: new items of its kind start that way.
- **Save the view's look as default**: render mode, projection and colouring.
- **Save the field colouring as default**.
- **Forget all defaults** goes back to the built-in ones.

Defaults are kept in `[defaults]` in settings.toml.

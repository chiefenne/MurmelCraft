Window commands: quit MurmelCraft, minimise the window, or turn full screen on and off.

<!-- more -->

## The window

- **Quit MurmelCraft** closes the window. On macOS the system's Quit does the same.
- **Minimise the window** puts it in the Dock or the task bar.
- **Full screen on or off** gives the whole screen to MurmelCraft.
